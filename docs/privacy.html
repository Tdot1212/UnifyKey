UnifyKey Privacy Policy

Effective: May 3, 2026

UnifyKey ("we," "the app") is a translation keyboard for iOS. This policy describes what data the app handles and where it goes. We've written it in plain English because vague legal boilerplate doesn't help anyone.

The short version

  • UnifyKey has no backend server. We never receive your data.
  • You provide your own API key for one of four AI providers (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google). When you translate, your text goes directly from your phone to your chosen provider — not through us.
  • API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only. They never leave your phone except when you make API calls.
  • Typing patterns the keyboard learns to improve predictions stay on your device.

Data we collect about you

None. UnifyKey does not have a server. We do not have user accounts. We do not have analytics. We do not see what you type, translate, copy, or send.

Data your API provider sees

When you tap the translate button, the text you typed (and optionally the message you copied to translate) is sent from your phone directly to the AI provider you selected. This includes:

  • DeepSeek — see platform.deepseek.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — see openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic — see anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google (Gemini) — see ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

What each provider does with your text is governed by their privacy policies, not ours. If you don't want a provider to see your text, don't use that provider. You can also stop typing into UnifyKey at any time and switch back to your default keyboard.

Data stored on your device

UnifyKey stores the following on your iPhone, accessible only to the UnifyKey app and its keyboard extension:

  • Your API key(s) — stored in the iOS Keychain with the strictest accessibility class (kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly), meaning the key cannot be backed up to iCloud Keychain and cannot be read while your phone is locked.
  • Typing patterns — words you type frequently and short phrases you commonly use. This data improves keyboard predictions and is stored locally only. You can clear it by deleting and reinstalling the app.
  • Settings — your selected language, target language, AI provider preference, and similar preferences. Stored on-device only.
  • Saved phrases — phrases you bookmark for reuse. Stored on-device only.

Clipboard access

UnifyKey reads your clipboard when the keyboard is shown, so it can offer to translate a message you just copied. Clipboard content is sent only to your selected AI provider when you choose to translate it. iOS shows a notification at the top of your screen each time UnifyKey reads the clipboard.

Full Access

To make API calls (which is the entire point of the app), UnifyKey's keyboard requires "Allow Full Access" in your iOS keyboard settings. Without it, no translation is possible. Full Access does not give us any data — it only allows the keyboard to make network requests using your API key. We have no server to send data to.

Children

UnifyKey is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone — child or adult — because we do not have a server.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add anything that handles user data differently (we don't plan to), we'll update this policy and bump the effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on our GitHub repo or reach out via the contact info on the App Store listing.
